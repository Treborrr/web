Cloud Security

Think about this: if someone walked off with your office computer tomorrow, what would your company lose? If the answer includes sales records, customer data, history, or accounting, you have a problem you don't know about yet. I fix it before it gets expensive.

01

Three ways companies lose everything

These aren't rare cases. They happen all the time, and they were almost always preventable.

/01

A single point of failure nobody sees coming

A damaged drive, a virus, a theft, a short circuit. Any one of these wipes out years of information in minutes if everything lives on a single machine. No backup means no recovery, and most companies find that out when it's already too late.

/02

Access that's never controlled

When everyone shares the same password or the same file, there's no way to know who saw what, who changed something, or who deleted an important record. A former employee who still has access to your system is a vulnerability that can end up costing more than everything you ever paid them.

/03

Operations stop right when you least expect it

If your system lives on a computer inside your store, a power outage, an internet failure, or a technical glitch brings your sales to a halt. Every hour your team can't work has a real cost that most people never calculate until after it happens.
