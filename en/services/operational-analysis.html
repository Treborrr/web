Operational Analysis

Most software projects fail because they get built before anyone understands the real problem. I do the opposite: I study your operation on the ground first, talk to your team, and see how information actually flows. Only then do I design the solution. That way you don't spend money on technology that misses what's actually hurting you.

01

Sound familiar?

If any of this sounds familiar, you already have your diagnosis.

/01

Some tasks eat hours, every single week

Closing the register, building reports, reconciling numbers. Someone on your team spends half a day on it, week after week. Multiply that by 52 weeks and you're paying an entire salary for something a well-built system would handle in seconds.

/02

Errors show up once it's too late to fix them

Data gets copied by hand from one spreadsheet to another, from paper to WhatsApp, from WhatsApp to a notebook. Every manual step is a chance to get something wrong. And errors don't announce themselves: you find them in inventory that doesn't add up, in an invoice issued wrong, in a customer complaint.

/03

You make decisions without clear numbers

The information exists, but it's scattered across notebooks, loose sheets, and different people's memory. To know how your business is really doing, someone has to piece it all together by hand. And by the time you have it, it's already changed.

02

What you get from the analysis

Not a report to file away. A map you can put to use the day you get it.

the main deliverable

The complete map of your operation, for the first time

I document exactly how information and money move through your company: who does what, when they do it, and with which tool. It's something most business owners have never seen laid out in one place before. Just having that clarity opens up decisions that weren't possible before.

Bottlenecks ranked by impact

Where you're losing time and money, prioritized. You'll know exactly what to fix first to feel the difference fastest.

A concrete action plan

What to build, in what order, and at what scope. No selling you technology you don't need or a bigger project than your company actually requires right now.

Real numbers, not optimistic guesses

Honest cost and timeline for every improvement. You decide with real information, not promises from someone trying to sell you something.

No obligation to keep working with me

The analysis stands on its own. If you decide to build the system with someone else, the map and the plan are entirely yours to keep.
